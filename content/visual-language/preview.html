Product identity stays official

Use Microsoft’s published Fabric SVGs for Fabric items. Keep their colors and geometry intact and place the item name beside them. Do not invent a Fabric ontology logo.

Abstract meaning stays explicit

Ontology: show named entities, relationships and definitions. Agent: show its role, inputs and outputs. Governance: show a permission boundary and allowed access. Avoid an unlabeled brain, diamond or gear.

Motion rules for our videos

  • Keep known objects in place; animate the new relationship or state.
  • Reveal one teaching point at a time, aligned to the narration.
  • Use deliberate holds. Motion ends before the viewer needs to read.
  • Arrows indicate a labelled relationship or direction; proximity alone implies neither dependency nor data flow.
  • At 1080p, start with 36–48px labels and check readability at 640×360. Fit checks include intermediate animation frames.

Prototype timings are editorial defaults for this example, not measurements from the Databricks reference. The preview uses deterministic frame-derived SVG/HTML state; it is not yet a Remotion integration.
