> Josh: Define a Fabric ontology in plain language.

> Guest: It's a shared model of the things the business cares about, what we know about them, how they connect, and where the supporting data lives.

> A Store is an entity type. A particular store is an instance. Region and priority are properties. “Store operates Refrigeration Unit” is a relationship. A data binding connects those concepts to actual source fields.

> We can also attach business rules and metrics. That lets a question refer to an established concept instead of asking every consumer to reconstruct its meaning from table names.
