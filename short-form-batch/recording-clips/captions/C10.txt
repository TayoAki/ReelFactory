That is the most persistent misconception we see and we really have to clear it up immediately. Fabric IQ is emphatically not a database. Okay, so it doesn't store data? It does not store your transactional data. Fabric IQ organizes the business context that hovers over the data you already have. If it doesn't hold data, what is it holding? Think of it as a semantic translation layer. Power BI provides your curated analytics and visuals. Your Lakehouse provides the raw storage, but the ontology living inside Fabric IQ defines the actual concepts and connections.
