Data without meaning is ingredients without a recipe
Original recording 06:21–06:43

Having raw data and an LLM without an ontology is essentially like having a massive commercial kitchen packed to the ceiling with premium ingredients. I like this analogy. But you have no recipe. You don't know the ratios, you don't know the cooking times, and you don't even know if you're supposed to be baking a wedding cake or making a beef stew. That is the functional reality for most enterprises today.
