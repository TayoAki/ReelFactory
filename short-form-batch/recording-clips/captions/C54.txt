Measure the whole reviewed workflow

Do not borrow percentage improvement benchmarks from other vendors, and do not even borrow benchmarks from other workflows within your own company. You have to test this system against your own current manual process for a highly specific decision. How do you structure that measurement? You start with a representative business question where you already definitively know the correct answer. You test whether the new AI workflow selects the right concepts, uses the right DAX calculations, strictly respects user access permissions, and returns evidence that a human operator can actually verify. And then you time it. Then you strictly time how long that entire process takes, compared to how long your human analysts take today. And crucially, you must include the time it takes the human operator to rigorously review and correct the AI's output in your final calculation.
