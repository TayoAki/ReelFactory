Keep monitoring conditions simple
Original recording 51:50–52:33
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

If you want to set up operational monitoring, the logical conditions you can set are currently quite primitive. The documentation notes that it struggles with combined and conditions and it struggles with aggregations. So I can't build a monitor that says alert me of the temperature drops and the store is priority and the total aggregated revenue is over $10 ,000. In the current preview, stringing complex, multi -variable logic like that into a passive monitor is highly problematic and likely to fail. You have to keep the monitoring conditions very simple and isolated. Furthermore, the operational agent operates on a fixed five -minute cycle. It is not sub -second real time. It only checks the condition every five minutes.
