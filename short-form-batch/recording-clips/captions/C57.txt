A merger is also a collision of definitions
Original recording 61:46–62:18
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

What happens during a major corporate merger? Oh, wow. When two massive global companies combine, is the future of M &A less about merging finances and entirely about whether their respective AI ontologies can successfully negotiate a shared reality? If company A's ontology defines risk or revenue in a way that fundamentally clashes with company B's ontology at a mathematical level, can the merger ever truly succeed without completely rewriting the central nervous system of one of the companies?
