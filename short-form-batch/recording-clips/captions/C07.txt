Fabric brings the workflow together
Original recording 08:15–08:42
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

Microsoft Fabric is an architectural attempt to bring the entire historically fragmented data workflow into one unified software as a service platform. And when we say workflow, we mean everything. The complete life cycle. Ingesting data from external sources, transforming and cleaning it, analyzing historical batches, real -time IOT information, training machine learning models, and finally serving up the visualizations in reporting tools.
