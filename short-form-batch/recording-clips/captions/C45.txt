Data agent versus operations agent
Original recording 48:44–49:09
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

The Fabric Data Agent is designed for the end -user. It provides the conversational analytics experience where a regional manager might sit down and ask everyday business questions about the data, utilizing the logic defined by the ontology. And third, the Operations Agent. The Operations Agent serves a completely different paradigm. Its job is not to answer questions, but to passively monitor a defined business process and recommend proactive responses.
