Your data stack still needs business meaning
Original recording 01:40–02:23
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

You are sitting at your desk. You have millions of data points at your fingertips. You've got tables, beautifully colored dashboards, the latest large language models. An absolute mountain of technology. A mountain. And yet, you still cannot get a straight, dependable answer to a painfully simple question like, which stores need my attention today? Yeah, it is sort of the defining paradox of the modern data stack. We have spent the last two decades focused almost entirely on the plumbing. How to move data faster, how to store more of it cheaper, or how to build better analytical engines. But the data itself. The raw data itself is often just a collection of totally disconnected facts.
