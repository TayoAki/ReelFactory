Start with one team and one decision
Original recording 60:11–60:53
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

Start incredibly small. Pick one single team, and one recurring high -value decision. The regional inventory review we discussed is a perfect example. Keep it contained. Connect only the specific data sources needed for that one decision. Define the handful of concepts that actually change the answer. And most importantly, aggressively test ambiguous requests and missing data scenarios to see exactly how the system fails under pressure. Do not try to boil the ocean and map the entire corporation's semantic logic on day one. Exactly. You only expand the scope when that first team can complete their workflow with dependably accurate answers, a highly manageable human review burden, and a fully understood, predictable operating cost.
