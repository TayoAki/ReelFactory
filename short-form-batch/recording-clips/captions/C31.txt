Data freshness depends entirely on the execution path. Because the AI doesn't know it's comparing apples to last week's oranges. Exactly. If the agent executes a direct query against a streaming KQL Eventhouse, that temperature data might literally be millisecond's old. Super fresh. But if it's simultaneously querying an imported Power BI model for the sales figures, those sales figures are only as fresh as the last time that specific Power BI model was scheduled to refresh. So if a job fails. If your scheduled refresh failed overnight, your data is 24 hours out of date. So a non -technical executive might look at the chat window, see an answer that looks completely uniform, highly confident, and beautifully formatted and have absolutely no idea that half the data informing that sentence is real time and half is a week old. That's scary. They might make a massive operational decision based on a temporal misalignment. This is why you cannot blindly trust the conversational output in the chat interface. You or your engineering team must rigorously check the source ingestion timestamps and verify the reporting periods.
