Reuse the Power BI model you already built
Original recording 18:19–18:48
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

The system is designed so you can generate a significant portion of your ontology directly from an existing power BI semantic model. That makes total sense because a semantic model already is a form of an ontology. It has the tables, the relationships, the business logic. How seamless is that translation process? The ontology agent essentially ingests the structure you've already built. The tables, columns, and established relationships in your Power BI model automatically provide the scaffolding for the ontologies, entities, and properties.
