Describing the rule is not executing it
Original recording 26:51–27:13
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

Writing the rule supplies the strategic context. Now, it is important to note that how that rule is actually executed, the specific database queries, the AI rights, to go fetch that data and test the condition, is handled by the query engines under the hood, which we need to discuss next. But the ontology is where the rule is permanently documented, linked, and standardized for the whole company.
