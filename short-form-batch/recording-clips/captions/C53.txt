Plan the old-experience migration
Original recording 56:54–57:19
ASR draft; technical-name spelling normalized.

Microsoft has established a hard retirement date for the old ontology experience. January 31st, 2027. That's a hard deadline. Moving to the new experience creates a completely separate new item in the workspace. This means data engineering teams will have to manually reconnect all their downstream consumers, rewrite their routing rules, and re -establish their automations. It's a hard migration cut over, and teams need to plan resources for it now.
