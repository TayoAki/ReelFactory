# Real-recording shorts: image-first production

Input is the five actual uploaded AAC parts, not the earlier script or our synthetic previews. Keep the original discussion audio. Two small fictional portrait placeholders signal the interview format without claiming speaker identity or lip-sync. Original recording speaker turns remain in the audio; portrait labels are not named as Josh/Guest.

Canvas: 1080×1920, 30fps. Source graphic language is the approved Fabric mint/emerald/teal editorial treatment: offwhite cards, soft borders/depth, filled symbols, serif concept labels. Portraits and diagrams are separate layers. No borrowed Databricks orange or Editor V1 red.

Layout: small brand/date eyebrow, strong editorial title, paired portrait strip, large full-width teaching diagram. Reserve a bottom caption band and social UI margins. Do not crop a 16:9 master. Source timing appears in the review pack; on-video context labels identify hypothetical examples and preview-specific claims when necessary.

Eight teaching families: context contrast, entity relationship, ownership/source link, definition versus execution boundary, ordered workflow, evidence/freshness review, hierarchy, and graph traversal. Generate one reusable master per family, parameterize content from the actual clip, derive coherent initial/build/settled images and rebuild as independent editable layers. Each clip stores its actual wording, selection reason and adaptation, not just a reusable template name.

Preserve reading holds. Selective gentle card entry, edge drawing and emphasis reveal the new concept. Captions are rendered from actual audio word timestamps, with corrected orthography for known technical names. No TTS, arbitrary footage, fabricated UI or output values. Source-claim review flags remain in the manifest; an export is a draft for review, not technical certification.
