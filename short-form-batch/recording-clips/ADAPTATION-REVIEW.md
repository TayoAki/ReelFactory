# Image-first adaptation review

## Generated target → editable reconstruction

Eight generated master images establish the white grid, Fabric mint/teal surfaces, serif hierarchy, soft depth and paired portrait strip. Every final diagram is rebuilt from separate cards, SVG symbols, exact labels and named connectors, rather than animating the master bitmap. Three state images per clip show the initial concept, relationship build and settled composition.

The vertical reconstruction increases label sizes and gives captions a fixed, separate band. It simplifies decorative chips from the generated masters so the actual speaker's chosen distinction remains legible. The generated ownership master incorrectly used a storefront for an ontology metric and green bars resembling Power BI branding; the editable version uses a generic measure symbol and the unchanged official semantic-model icon. These changes correct semantics rather than copying generated errors.

The original style anchor is reference-library/frames/graphic/0003510.jpg; the interview composition anchor is reference-library/frames/full/0001245.jpg. Reading direction is distinct from reveal order: cards appear at matching recorded keywords where found; the first anchor is introduced within the first second, and unmatched concepts use an editorial fallback. Exact timing provenance is recorded per layer.

## Why these diagrams

- Contrasts retain the shared question/value while two interpretations are compared. An arrow between alternatives would suggest false causation.
- Type/instance and hierarchy clips label their different kinds of objects; synonyms explicitly resolve to one entity rather than creating a second one.
- Ownership diagrams label source versus consumer and direct references back toward the source. They do not imply copying DAX.
- Boundary diagrams leave responsibilities separate. No success tick, executed notification, or security isolation is invented.
- Workflows expose intermediate review steps. The source-routes clip is parallel categories, with no ordered arrows.
- Evidence clips ask what to verify instead of showing a fake successful chart.
- The recall path is a hypothetical chain of named entities; it does not claim SQL fails or a real recall query ran.

An authentic product recording is the stronger alternative for proving clicks, source permissions or output correctness. These audio-only concept clips cannot prove those outcomes. The source interview contains dated preview claims and hypotheticals; review flags stay with the drafts.

## Review status

Image layouts were sampled visually and checked for card overflow. Caption bands are separate from diagrams and portraits. Technical exports receive duration, codec, dimensions and full decode checks. ASR captions still need human proofreading; neither export checks nor automatic layout checks certify editorial quality or human approval. Portraits remain static fictional placeholders.
