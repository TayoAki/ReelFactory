What changed

  • Result in the opening: the real 3,963,898-request dataset and the category ranking are visible immediately.
  • A continuous investigation: category → date window → hourly noise → Sunday → monthly heat → the small-category contrast.
  • Readable evidence: original charts fill the screen, with stable colors, labels and geometry.
  • Purposeful motion: reveals follow the explanation; the earlier crop-swapping cadence is removed.
