## Databricks Genie One FAQ: Your Enterprise AI Questions Answered

Expand All

### What is Databricks Genie One?

Genie One is a place where business users can explore analytical dashboards, converse with their data and interact with custom-built apps, powered by AI. It gives knowledge workers a single, governed entry point to ask questions, get context-aware answers and take action without needing to write code or queries. Company branding and a unified home make it a natural front door for data and AI across an organization.

### Who is Genie One built for?

Any team that relies on data to make decisions — including sales, finance, marketing, operations and supply chain — can get value from Genie One. Sales teams track pipeline and quota attainment, finance teams query budget variances and margins, marketing teams analyze campaign performance, and supply chain teams interrogate inventory and supplier data. If your team makes decisions with data, Genie is for you.

### How can I get access to Genie One?

Genie One is available to all Databricks users. Ask your admin to assign the consumer entitlement role to the users and teams that require access, which unlocks the streamlined Genie One experience for them.

### How is Genie One priced?

Genie One follows Databricks' usage-based pricing model, so you only pay for the products you use, billed at per-second granularity. This keeps spending predictable and in check as adoption grows across teams. Full pricing details across products and SKUs are available on the [Databricks pricing page](https://www.databricks.com/product/pricing).

### How does Genie One handle security and governance?

Governance is built into Genie from the ground up via Unity Catalog. Permissions are enforced at the data level, so users only see what they're authorized to see, and every action Genie takes is scoped, auditable and compliant by design. This makes it possible to move from small AI experiments to large-scale trusted deployment with confidence.

### What data sources can Genie One connect to?

Genie connects natively to your Databricks Lakehouse, and to the tools your teams already live in — Slack, Jira, Google Drive, Salesforce and more. With over 100 connectors and MCP support, it reaches across your SaaS apps, operational systems, documents and dashboards. Ask a question in Slack and get an answer grounded in your lakehouse, or trigger a Jira update from a Genie insight. All connections are managed through Unity AI Gateway so access stays governed end to end.

### How do I know Genie One's answers are trustworthy?

Genie's answers are grounded in your own governed data and knowledge, not a generic model. Genie continuously extracts and updates business knowledge from assets like notebooks and dashboards, ranks them by how authoritative and widely used they are, and only serves information that respects the original permissions, so nothing leaks to the wrong people. This architecture lets Genie deliver more accurate and faster answers.

### What's the difference between Genie One and Genie Code?

Genie One is an AI coworker for business users, providing a single entry point for interacting with data and AI without navigating technical concepts like compute, queries, models or notebooks. Genie Code is the AI coding and data assistant for developers and technical practitioners in the Databricks workspace. The two products serve different audiences: Genie One for business decision-making and Genie Code for hands-on technical work.

### What is Databricks Genie One?

Genie One is a place where business users can explore analytical dashboards, converse with their data and interact with custom-built apps, powered by AI. It gives knowledge workers a single, governed entry point to ask questions, get context-aware answers and take action without needing to write code or queries. Company branding and a unified home make it a natural front door for data and AI across an organization.

### Who is Genie One built for?

Any team that relies on data to make decisions — including sales, finance, marketing, operations and supply chain — can get value from Genie One. Sales teams track pipeline and quota attainment, finance teams query budget variances and margins, marketing teams analyze campaign performance, and supply chain teams interrogate inventory and supplier data. If your team makes decisions with data, Genie is for you.

### How can I get access to Genie One?

Genie One is available to all Databricks users. Ask your admin to assign the consumer entitlement role to the users and teams that require access, which unlocks the streamlined Genie One experience for them.

### How is Genie One priced?

Genie One follows Databricks' usage-based pricing model, so you only pay for the products you use, billed at per-second granularity. This keeps spending predictable and in check as adoption grows across teams. Full pricing details across products and SKUs are available on the [Databricks pricing page](https://www.databricks.com/product/pricing).

### How does Genie One handle security and governance?

Governance is built into Genie from the ground up via Unity Catalog. Permissions are enforced at the data level, so users only see what they're authorized to see, and every action Genie takes is scoped, auditable and compliant by design. This makes it possible to move from small AI experiments to large-scale trusted deployment with confidence.

### What data sources can Genie One connect to?

Genie connects natively to your Databricks Lakehouse, and to the tools your teams already live in — Slack, Jira, Google Drive, Salesforce and more. With over 100 connectors and MCP support, it reaches across your SaaS apps, operational systems, documents and dashboards. Ask a question in Slack and get an answer grounded in your lakehouse, or trigger a Jira update from a Genie insight. All connections are managed through Unity AI Gateway so access stays governed end to end.

### How do I know Genie One's answers are trustworthy?

Genie's answers are grounded in your own governed data and knowledge, not a generic model. Genie continuously extracts and updates business knowledge from assets like notebooks and dashboards, ranks them by how authoritative and widely used they are, and only serves information that respects the original permissions, so nothing leaks to the wrong people. This architecture lets Genie deliver more accurate and faster answers.

### What's the difference between Genie One and Genie Code?

Genie One is an AI coworker for business users, providing a single entry point for interacting with data and AI without navigating technical concepts like compute, queries, models or notebooks. Genie Code is the AI coding and data assistant for developers and technical practitioners in the Databricks workspace. The two products serve different audiences: Genie One for business decision-making and Genie Code for hands-on technical work.
