# Scene-by-scene reference review

Full-file automated cut detection at reduced resolution plus manual inspection of 29 timestamped samples per downloaded video and available auto captions. Semantic ranges below are approximate editorial scene ranges, not frame-exact EDL boundaries. No claim of exhaustive frame-by-frame human review or audio-stem measurement.

## What is complete

11 TikTok videos acquired through ScrapeCreators and visually sampled. Seven available auto-caption tracks retained. Two YouTube videos have metadata only; playable footage was not available through ScrapeCreators and direct retrieval was rejected by the environment proxy. No visual conclusions are attributed to those two videos.

The supplied nine scripts are adapted into native NextPhase Editor V1 projects under `../videos/`. Production uses original narration, official Microsoft images and original public-data charts. Creator footage is research-only. The source references mostly use presenters and live screen recordings; these first drafts have no supplied presenter recording and are source-based adaptations, not identical reproductions.

## Corrections to the supplied inspiration notes

- The SQL interview reference is a roughly 176-second masking tutorial. The four-option quiz is an original adaptation, not an observed source format.
- The career reference is a single roughly eight-second shot with an accumulating five-item list, not a visual myth/reveal.
- The voucher reference is a roughly eight-second event montage, not a 70-second listicle. Its displayed deadline is historical.
- The first Fabric video opens with a question about whether Fabric is a new Power BI; its visible opening does not establish a most-searched claim.
- The Claude + Power BI source demonstrates a Modeling MCP extension; it does not verify Fabric data-agent behavior or cost.
