Claude Dashboards can connect to Databricks and build a dashboard from a question.

Anthropic announced it on October eighth. The announcement describes a beta on paid plans.

Step one. Connect a supported data platform, such as Databricks, Snowflake or Big Query.

Step two. Ask a specific question in plain English, like this week's signups compared with last month's.

Claude retrieves the data and builds the dashboard. This is a source based walkthrough of the announced feature.

Step three. Inspect the answer. The announcement says you can click a number to see its query.

Each chart also shows when its data was last refreshed.

Enterprise admins must enable the feature. Check access and verify the query before sharing the result.
