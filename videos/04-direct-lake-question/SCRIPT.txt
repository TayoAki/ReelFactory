A Fabric interview question. Your sales data is stored as Delta tables in OneLake.

You want import style query performance without copying the entire dataset into the model.

Which storage mode fits? Import? Direct Query? Or Direct Lake?

Pause here and choose your answer.

Direct Lake. It loads the columns needed for queries from Delta tables in OneLake.

A refresh updates metadata. It does not import the entire dataset again.

Here is the detail to check. Direct Lake on SQL can fall back to Direct Query.

Direct Lake on OneLake does not use that fallback. Their security behavior differs too.

Read the comparison before choosing a mode. The correct design depends on the source and permissions.
