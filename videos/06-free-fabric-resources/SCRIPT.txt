Three free Microsoft resources for learning Fabric. Save these official pages.

One. A Fabric trial gives eligible users sixty days to explore the platform.

Use it to practice lakehouses, ingestion and reporting. Check the current trial limits before you start.

Two. The DP seven hundred practice assessment is available free on Microsoft Learn.

Use the feedback to find gaps. It is a study tool, not a guarantee of an exam result.

Three. The exam sandbox lets you explore the exam interface before booking your test.

Start with the certification page and follow its official links. Keep the resources together for your next study session.
