Right. Start with four entity types: Customer, Order, Shipment, and Warehouse. A particular shipment, like Shipment seventeen, is an instance. Its status and promised delivery time are properties.

Now name the relationships. A customer places an order. A shipment fulfills that order. A warehouse dispatches the shipment. Those labels explain what each connection means.

But where do the actual values come from?

Data bindings connect those business definitions to real sources. For example, orders can come from a lakehouse, while shipment updates come from an eventhouse. The ontology adds shared meaning; it doesn't become a second copy of the database.
