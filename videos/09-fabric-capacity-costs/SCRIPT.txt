Pausing Fabric capacity does not stop the charge for a reservation you already bought.

A reservation discount applies hour by hour. Unused hours do not carry forward.

So check how your reservation benefit is being used before you pause capacity.

There is a second check. Remaining cumulative overages and smoothed operations are added to your Azure bill when you pause.

Use the Capacity Metrics app to inspect the capacity's usage.

Before pausing, check the reservation and pending usage together. A green pause button does not erase an existing commitment.
