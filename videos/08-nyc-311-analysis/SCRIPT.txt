I queried a year of New York's three one one requests. Almost four million records.

The window is October first, twenty twenty five, through September thirtieth, twenty twenty six.

The biggest category is illegal parking. Six hundred eleven thousand, two hundred eighty two requests.

Residential noise is next, with more than four hundred thirty two thousand requests.

Those residential noise requests peak in the ten p m hour. Sunday is the busiest day.

Heat and hot water requests show a different pattern. January is far busier than August.

At the other end of the list, exactly one request is filed under trapping pigeon.

These are request counts, not unique incidents. The queries and results are saved so you can reproduce the charts.
